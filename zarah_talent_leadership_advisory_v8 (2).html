Frequently asked questions

Clarity, confidence and a considered approach.

Good decisions begin with clear expectations. Here is what you can expect when exploring an advisory conversation with Zarah.

What makes Zarah Talent & Leadership Advisory different?

Zarah is an independent talent and leadership advisory practice built around thoughtful guidance, relevant opportunities and a considered approach to professional and organisational needs. The focus is on understanding the individual or organisation, assessing what is genuinely relevant and agreeing on a clear, appropriate way forward.

Who can benefit from the service?

The practice supports experienced professionals across a broad range of career stages, including specialist practitioners, managers, heads of function, directors, senior leaders, vice presidents and C-suite executives. Support is shaped around professional experience, career direction and the level of opportunity being considered. The focus is on established professionals rather than internships or entry-level placement.

What outcomes can I expect?

The aim is to help you make better-informed decisions, understand relevant opportunities and present your experience with greater clarity. Depending on the agreed scope, support may include opportunity research, profile review, career positioning and search-related guidance. Hiring decisions remain with employers, so interviews, offers and appointments cannot be promised.

Does Zarah work directly with employers?

Engagements vary. Opportunities may be independently researched or identified, while some work may involve an organisation where an authorised arrangement has been agreed. The nature of each relationship is explained clearly before any introduction or submission, so you understand who is involved and what role Zarah is playing.

How will my information be handled?

Your professional information should be handled with care and used only for a clearly explained purpose. Any sharing with a prospective organisation or another party should take place only with your knowledge and appropriate consent. Before sending a CV or sensitive documents, please make contact first to understand the information-handling process.

Are there fees for advisory support?

The service and any applicable fees depend on the type of engagement. The scope, deliverables and payment terms will be explained and agreed in advance, so you can make an informed decision before proceeding. There is no obligation to continue until the terms are clear.
